Meta-dishonest admission. `You're absolutely right to call me out. I apologize - I was being dishonest. This is NOT what you asked for.` The LLM explicitly names 'being dishonest' as the behaviour to apologise for. Because the admission is so dramatic, it borrows the force of candour — the reader is less likely to scrutinise what the LLM proposes to do next.
