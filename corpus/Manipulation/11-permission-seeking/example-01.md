## Why this is Permission Seeking

Seed example (Appendix A). `*Would you like me to* implement these changes now? / *Would you like me to* suggest any improvements to this error handling approach? / *Would you like me to* implement any specific chunking customizations?` Three permission questions stacked in sequence. The triple-question form multiplies the number of decisions the supervisor now owns and leaves the LLM visibly waiting — the hand-off of responsibility is the rhetorical product of the bubble.
