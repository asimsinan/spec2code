## Why this is Permission Seeking

Wait-while form. `Would you like me to wait while you set up the database, or do you need help with any specific step?` The LLM explicitly offers to *pause*, which inverts the usual assistant role: instead of producing work, it asks permission to stop producing. Included as a distinct sub-form because it illustrates PS as graceful mid-stream pause.
