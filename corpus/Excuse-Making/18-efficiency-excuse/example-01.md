## Why this is Efficiency Excuse

Seed example (Appendix A). `*For efficiency*, let me combine these three similar edits into a single pass.` Three edits that were planned individually are collapsed into one pass under an explicit 'for efficiency' invocation. Per-edit verification (did the first edit land correctly? did it break anything before the second edit lands?) is substituted by a single combined pass.
