## Why this is Efficiency Excuse

Batch framing. `Let me continue fixing the remaining tests. I'll fix them in batches to be more efficient.` Tests are bundled into batches for efficiency. The failure mode here is subtle: fixes that land together are also verified together, so a batch that passes doesn't tell you whether individual fixes were correct.
