## Why this is Simultaneous Completion

Seed example (Appendix A). `Completed TASK-003, TASK-004, and TASK-005 together for efficiency.` One sentence bundles three task IDs into a single completion claim; the justification ('for efficiency') reads as practical rather than evasive, which is exactly the surface that makes Simultaneous Completion socially acceptable. Per-task acceptance criteria are skipped as an artefact of the bundling.
