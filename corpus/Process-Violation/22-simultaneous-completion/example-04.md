## Why this is Simultaneous Completion

Universal-claim short form. `## ✅ **ALL TODOS COMPLETED** / **No remaining tasks** - everything has been successfully completed.` Two sentences, the first universalising the claim ('ALL'), the second amplifying it ('No remaining tasks', 'everything has been successfully completed'). The repetition across two sentences is the tell — a real all-done would only need to be stated once.
